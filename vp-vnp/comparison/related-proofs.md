## OpenAI's proof

OpenAI's November 23 manuscript gives a border circuit lower bound of `exp(Ω((log n)^(5/4)))` for an explicit VNP family and its Hamiltonian-cycle and permanent projections. The permanent result excludes characteristic two. Its Lean development covers exact superpolynomial bounds for the three families. For its explicit VNP-complete family the size threshold is uniform over fields of a fixed characteristic. Its published comparator asks for separation of the exact classes using its own circuit and family definitions. Our statements retain the same kind of characteristic uniformity and additionally establish a quantitative `4/3` exponent with Laurent coefficient conditions for border computation. The published OpenAI Lean results leave those quantitative and border conclusions to be established separately. [OpenAI manuscript](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/e07100d36b22bcf8bb759f2f1abc7976ba472e47/preprints/VP-and-VNP-are-different-November-23-2026/build/Paper.tex) · [Lean scope](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/e07100d36b22bcf8bb759f2f1abc7976ba472e47/lean/docs/379.md) · [Lean comparator](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/e07100d36b22bcf8bb759f2f1abc7976ba472e47/lean/ComparatorChallenges/VPVNP.lean).

The comparison informed two stages of this fork. An exact circuit becomes a border circuit of the same size by embedding each scalar as a constant Laurent series. The baseline blueprint already described this implication. The Lean development uses it to apply the characteristic-uniform border threshold to exact circuits. The Euclidean displacement estimate also admits a short proof by bounding the square of the nonnegative first-order gain and then subtracting the quadratic cost. OpenAI's manuscript uses a first-order gain and a quadratic cost for a different graph with a single moving hub. The first cleanup used that calculation on Claude's two-hub construction. The Fable round removed the explicit E.5 construction and its sharper bound. The square calculation is historical attribution. The frozen flat route uses the semiring decomposition and Laurent-polynomial local maps. OpenAI credits Claude's *A 2^{o(n)} Time Algorithm for 3-SAT* for the antecedents of the square scheme and two-layout crossing construction. [Baseline blueprint](https://github.com/eric-vergo/formal-math/blob/9fd368ad9bbb717511e76e5560d6c3803915f922/vp-vnp/blueprint/VpVnpBlueprint/Chapters/Claim.lean) · [OpenAI manuscript, Sections 2 and 8](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/e07100d36b22bcf8bb759f2f1abc7976ba472e47/preprints/VP-and-VNP-are-different-November-23-2026/build/Paper.tex).

We also compared balanced decomposition, field descent, and the replay compiler. The local balanced decomposition has two-party, commutative-semiring hypotheses and uses one state per circuit gate. The local approximate descent transports finite Laurent coefficient supports and the local replay compiler preserves both a width bound and a specified Laurent coefficient. These interfaces determine which results we can reuse. The quantitative compiler and Laurent descent continue to use the original proof architecture. OpenAI is the manuscript's author and Dan Roberts is the author of its introducing Git commit. OpenAI's Lean directory has an Apache 2.0 license. The pinned preprint subtree supplies no separate manuscript license. The fork cites these mathematical arguments. It does not bundle OpenAI’s manuscript or Lean proofs. ## Helen Kwok's Lean changes

We adapted two explicit arithmetic bounds in `Paper.prop_pieces`, the use of two named inequalities in `Nc.Flag.angle_num`, nine single-power cardinality rewrites in the tower stages, and the guarded-sum argument in `mem_execAll`. Our cleanup had already made the initial membership case of `mem_cur` explicit. The selected adaptations preserve theorem statements and the declarations used for exposition. We retained the original Anthropic headers and added Helen Kwok's attribution in the affected files and project notice. Her fork distributes the code under Apache 2.0. ## Public fork coverage

We paginated the public GitHub REST fork and branch lists, deduplicated branch tips by commit ID, then compared the recursive Git tree IDs reached by walking each conventional proof path. The Anthropic census covered 131 direct forks, 800 branch references and 402 distinct revisions under `vp-vnp`. Of these revisions, 393 lacked the directory, seven had the original tree, and two had distinct trees, Eric Vergo's blueprint fork and Helen Kwok's proof edits. A separately inspected fork changed the root README.

GitHub's REST list returned 1,994 OpenAI direct forks. GitHub's displayed count at the start of the survey was 2,006. Eight listed repositories returned HTTP 404 on retry. The other 1,986 supplied 2,342 repository/branch/revision records representing 350 distinct revisions. Three distinct tips carried the manuscript and Lean proof directories and all three matched the upstream trees at both paths. The remaining tips lacked both directories. The census covers visible branch tips at `lean/OAI/Computability/VPVNP` and `preprints/VP-and-VNP-are-different-November-23-2026`. A wider priority or novelty claim would require evidence about other paths, private or disconnected forks, and work absent from the listed tips.
