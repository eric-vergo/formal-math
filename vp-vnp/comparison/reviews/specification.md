# Source audit of the frozen specification and mathematical scope

Independent reading of Statement.lean, Comparator/VpVnp/{Challenge,Solution}.lean, comparator.json, Paper/Fields.lean and Paper/Approximate.lean. Checked the former files and paper.pdf byte-for-byte with git diff --exit-code against 9fd368ad. Neither agreement of source files nor a kernel proof certifies the intended English interpretation.

- Statement.Computes is existential in a gate sequence; only its first s entries are considered. At each position, a gate must be an input, a field constant or the sum/product of two strictly earlier positions. The output must occur before s. A one-gate sequence computes a constant, and any monomial is constructible with enough gates. Thus computations are nonempty for positive budgets and s=0 cannot make a positive claim vacuously about a computed output. Counting inputs and constants is part of this circuit convention. The equality tested is equality of polynomials, including over finite fields.
- Statement.perm sums products of independent variables over all permutations. Statement.hc restricts permutations by IsCycleOn Set.univ. For m≥2 a cyclic permutation exists and contributes a distinct monomial whose coefficient is one. Distinct permutations give different exponent vectors. A field has one different from zero. Thus neither use of a field in the eventual lower bound makes the target a default zero polynomial. Small m is outside the asserted range when the threshold is increased.
- Statement.Beyond uses strict real comparison with c times a positive power of log base two. MainTheorem and MainTheoremApprox demand c>0, chosen once before the characteristic. Each characteristic has one finite natural threshold before the quantifier over fields of that characteristic. For composite nonzero p no field satisfies CharP F p. The Solution adapters explicitly discard those p via CharP.char_is_prime_or_zero. Characteristic zero and all primes remain substantive; the exception p=2 restricts the permanent only.
- Statement.Approximates requires the same circuit budget for q over LaurentSeries F; for every exponent vector, all strictly negative Laurent coefficients vanish and coefficient zero equals that of f. The positive coefficients are free. Choosing the constant-in-epsilon polynomial embeds ordinary exact computations, while an unrelated zero polynomial fails the constant-term equations for nonzero f. There is no implicit analytic topology or textual equivalence to every convention for border complexity in this machine check.
- Statement.Family demands a finite variable type at each n. Statement.VP and VPBar each require one polynomial bound, simultaneously, on variable counts, total degree and circuit sizes across all n. Statement.VNP selects Boolean witness bits and a family of polynomials in visible plus witness variables already in VP; the variable-count condition bounds the number of witness bits. The identity is polynomial equality. Setting zero witness bits explains VP inclusion. Paper.vp_subset_vnp supplies that inclusion; Paper.hc_mem_VNP supplies the explicit family used for both exported failures of the reverse inclusion (one exact, one approximate). The internal real separation Paper.cor_classes uses the permanent by Ryser's formula and is a separate paper-facing result.
- Solution.toNc/ofNc preserve each variable type, finite-type witness and polynomial. The five judged names are listed explicitly in comparator.json, which allows propext, Quot.sound and Classical.choice. It has an empty definition_names array. Preservation of the shared definitions is separately checked by the file diff. Challenge contains the five deliberately unproved challenge statements; Solution imports Statement and Paper.Approximate, not Challenge.
- Paper.thm_allfields first picks c=1/1600^2, obtains an algebraically closed field in each admissible characteristic, uses complete grids for HC and PERM (the latter outside characteristic two), and transfers an exact computation into that field. Paper.thm_main specializes this result to ℝ and p=0, so its kernel route also includes the HC component packaged in the general theorem. Paper.thm_approx uses the same characteristic-level threshold strategy for Laurent approximations; lem_transfer_approx returns a Laurent polynomial over the auxiliary field with the prescribed nonpositive coefficients. Both approximation exports project the same conjunction, so their closures also include the VNP membership/separation component. A kernel dependency records occurrence in a compiled proof, not logical necessity. Actual per-export closure and cited paths must be checked against the final compiled inventory.

This covers formal arithmetic circuit and class statements. It gives neither a Boolean circuit separation nor uniform/executable algorithms or an explicit threshold. The semantics of Mathlib's foundational types, the kernel/exporter/replay checks, and the relation of these formal definitions to a reader's preferred mathematical convention are distinct trust questions.
