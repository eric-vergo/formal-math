# Cross-module duplicate and near-duplicate review

The baseline's largest exact duplicated body was 14 lines (the packet count sum bound). This is distinct from repeated *parts* of long proofs. For an independent second pass, I scanned the current source, masking comments, standardizing whitespace and recurring local hypothesis names, and matched overlapping five-line windows between different modules. I reviewed the strongest results in their surrounding statements. Window sizes are search cues, not official inventory counts.

1. Paper.Next.prop_next_round and Paper.ApproxRound.prop_approx_acc_round_num (declaration namespace Paper in both files). The longest surviving common sequence was 21 normalized lines. Both rounds already use round_plain_bounds. The exponent facts also recur in their two iteration theorems prop_next and prop_approx_acc_next. Commit 60aa408 establishes them once as Paper.round_exponent_bounds and uses pow_succ' for the shared multiplication identity. The exact theorem has one tree and one program. The approximation proof needs a second tree, an explicit span bound, a rescaling exponent and a Laurent evaluation bound. These explain its distinct output and time/size estimates.
2. Nc.Tow.Ever2, Nc.Tow.nR_mid3 and Nc.Tow.Load2, Nc.Tow.num_mid_le: the same relay-target uniqueness argument at stage three, a directed step bounded by stage two. Commit dadcd28 puts this statement beside the numeric bound as Nc.Tow.num_mid3_le_mid2. The optional-validity wrapper uses it directly; the four-stage bound composes it with the stage-two estimate. Both modules independently elaborated. The same review exposed a repeated case analysis in Nc.Tow.nEnd_eq. In e8deb44 the load indicator is moved inside the finite sum and the proof considers the two endpoints and the remaining vertices once each.
3. Nc.Uni.EvThm2, Nc.Uni.EvOK.cons/ite and Nc.Uni.EvThm3, Nc.Uni.EvOK.run. Sequential operand simulation exhibits the same heap-extension monotonicity and register-representation proofs. The cons clause builds a cell and returns, ite tests one output and evaluates one branch, and run decodes and invokes the evaluated code. Maintaining the heap/cost bound for the third evaluation is specific to run. Common opcode decoding has already been factored out, and the remaining repeated applications depend on different register positions and intermediate heaps.
4. NS1.Comp, NS1.Does.ite_of_gives and Nc.Grp.Lang, NS1.Does.ite_nil. Both compose evaluator run lists and multiply weights, but Gives guarantees one deterministic value with total weight one. Does.ite_nil has a potentially arbitrary input weight a and maps every resulting pair to a multiple by a. Their identical middle cost calculations do not permit substituting one theorem for the other. In the related Nc.Acc.CompK.ite_of_gives, the finite Laurent sum and valuation guarantee are extra proof obligations.
5. Paper.Circuit, Paper.lem_circuit and Paper.ApproxScales, Paper.lem_circuit_approx. The initial choice of universal projection, program and scale threshold, and the conversion of a circuit into a bound on the program share a setup. The central construction is genuinely different: a circuit over a field with a polynomial identity in the former, a Laurent computation with prescribed low coefficients in the latter. Reusing the existing lem_circuit_universal and lem_circuit_program preserves that distinction. NS1.ApproxRound.stepA_kv and NS1.Step.stepF_ev have an analogous common application of an evaluator to a list; one proves a Laurent valuation/approximation and the other exact execution.
6. Nc.Tow.Load0 vs Nc.Tow.Load2 target classification. tgR_edge_succ rules out initial packets using the impossibility of Fin.succ = 0, tgR_seg enumerates two distinct false-channel endpoints, and tgR_mid enumerates true-channel predecessor stages, including a conditional bypass. These conclusions concern different target constructors. A common bulk simp on all constructors would hide the dependencies on nontrivial stage and endpoint distinctions. Nc.Tow.seg_zero and seg_le then consume the classification separately for an inactive type and a positive load bound.
7. Nc.Grp.GroupProg.Nc.Grp.ex_ins: an intra-proof duplicate outside the cross-module matching was removed in 5776f73. A grade-school distributive identity had been proved five times with distinct arguments; the bounds for trie lookup length were repeated in the two equality instructions. The actual instruction implementations remain separate.
8. The baseline's exact packet-count, opcode inversion, conditional evaluation and common encoded-bit application clusters have named shared implementations in the cleanup. Short identical bodies can still prove different statements. NS1.KT.All.imp and All.map are the direct tree inductions for a predicate on the same tree and a predicate on a mapped tree, respectively. The matching proof scripts alone cannot justify deleting either interface.
